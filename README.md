# AlgoFeed: Open-Source Humanitarian & Social Logistics OS

> **The first open-source, modular operating system designed to optimize humanitarian supply chains, food rescue, and social care distribution through AI-driven routing.**

---

## 🌟 Overview & Mission

AlgoFeed bridges the gap between donors, humanitarian NGOs, retail chains, and municipal services. It provides an enterprise-grade logistics backend as a **Digital Public Good**, while offering commercial integrations to ensure long-term project self-sustainability.

---

## 🏗️ Open-Core Business Architecture

AlgoFeed operates under an **Open-Core Dual-Licensing / Freemium Model**:
## 💼 Commercial & Enterprise Services (B2B / B2G)

While the Core platform remains free for non-profits, AlgoFeed provides monetization streams through high-value enterprise features:

### 1. 📊 Corporate ESG & CSR Reporting Engine (B2B)
* **Target:** Retail chains, pharmaceutical corporations, FMCG manufacturers.
* **Value Prop:** Automates real-time, audit-ready EU CSRD-compliant reports (CO₂ reduction, diverted waste value, social impact metrics).
* **Pricing:** Monthly / Annual SaaS Subscription per facility or warehouse.

### 2. 🔄 Automated ERP / Supply Chain Integration (B2B)
* **Target:** Supermarkets and food distributors.
* **Value Prop:** Direct API middleware connecting inventory systems (SAP, Oracle) to automatically flag surplus goods and trigger food rescue workflows.
* **Pricing:** Enterprise Integration Fee + Volume-based API usage.

### 3. 🏛️ Municipal & Emergency Operations Center (B2G)
* **Target:** Local governments, disaster management bodies, municipal care networks.
* **Value Prop:** High-availability city-wide dashboard for crisis routing, social care time-window optimization, and multi-fleet management.
* **Pricing:** Annual B2G licensing and custom infrastructure deployment.

---

## 🔁 Cross-Subsidization Impact Model

> **100% of commercial profits generated from Enterprise SaaS licensing are reinvested into maintaining, developing, and hosting the free Open-Source Community Core for NGOs worldwide.
